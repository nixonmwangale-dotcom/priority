Artificial Intelligence

We demystify AI for everyday community use — from smallholder farms to secondary school classrooms — so that people can put these tools to work rather than be left behind by them.

Focus area 03

Youth Empowerment

Young people are not a program beneficiary category to us — they are co-designers of Hatua Mkononi's work. We invest in mentorship, entrepreneurship, and digital skills that open real pathways to income and leadership.

Entrepreneurship Leadership Career mentorship Digital skills Innovation challenges Volunteer opportunities

Success story: Wanjiru's Studio

After completing our Digital Skills track, 19-year-old Wanjiru launched a graphic design studio now serving 12 small businesses across her county.

A team of five youth innovators built a low-cost soil-sensor prototype during our Innovation Challenge, now piloting with 40 local farms.
