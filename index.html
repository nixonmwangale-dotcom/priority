Who we are

Building a movement, one community step at a time.

Hatua Mkononi is a community-driven organization working across Kenya and beyond to place practical tools directly into people's hands — literally, "step by hand." We believe lasting change happens when communities themselves lead the way, supported by the right knowledge, technology, and partnerships.

Our vision is a generation of resilient, innovative communities where no one is left behind by the pace of technological and environmental change.

Our mission is to equip communities — especially young people — with practical skills in Artificial Intelligence, climate resilience, and leadership, delivered through hands-on, locally led programs.

  • 🤝 Community-centered design in everything we build
  • 🌍 Inclusion at the core — gender, geography, and ability
  • 💡 Innovation grounded in local realities, not imported blueprints
