Who we are

Five priorities, one shared strategy.

PRIORITY FIVE INITIATIVE equips communities in Bungoma, Nairobi, and beyond with the knowledge, skills, and technology — and provides a platform for the partnerships — needed to build a more inclusive, resilient, and sustainable future.

Our vision is a society in which every person, regardless of origin or circumstance, has access to safety, opportunity, and a voice in shaping a sustainable future.

Our mission is to pool together people, ideas, and resources across communities to deliver practical, dignity-centered support to refugees, accelerate local climate action, expand responsible use of artificial intelligence, and empower young people through education and opportunity.

  • 🤝 Community-centered design in everything we build
  • 🌍 Inclusion at the core — displacement status, gender, geography, and ability
  • 💡 Innovation grounded in local realities, not imported blueprints
